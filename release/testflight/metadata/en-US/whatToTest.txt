What to test

Choose Bluetooth device to open the device picker. This fixture filters for devices named hito or Hito and requests the Hito service UUID. Select your own compatible test wallet; the fixture connects to its GATT server and reports connection events. Other websites must request their own filters and services. Pairing alone does not grant a website access.

Tap Scan NFC and hold a compatible NDEF tag near the top of your iPhone. The console shows record types and payloads. Stop the scan before switching tasks. To write, use your own writable test tag: enter text and tap Write NFC text, or test a binary record. Writing replaces tag content. This fixture does not read payment cards or emulate NFC cards.

If an action fails, read the console error, check iOS Bluetooth or NFC permissions and the site controls, then retry in the foreground. After navigation or backgrounding, request permission again. The simulator cannot verify radio or tag operations. Test with real hardware; cancel pickers and check that denied access stays denied.
